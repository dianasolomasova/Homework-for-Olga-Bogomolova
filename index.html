Dialogue: A Big Change

Mark: Anna, I heard something. Is it true? Are you really leaving?

Anna: Yes, Mark. I told my boss yesterday that I'm quitting.

Mark: Wow. How did it go?

Anna: It went fine. But he's not very happy. He will probably not hire anyone new, so it's going to be hard for them without me.

Mark: That's true. You do a lot. So, what's next?

Anna: I found a new job. I will have new responsibilities. My main task is to make a landing page more user-friendly and to encourage companies to buy a subscription.

Mark: That sounds interesting. Will you work from the office?

Anna: I hope I will work remotely. But there's one problem. My new team consists of men only, and I'm not happy.

Mark: Really? Why not?

Anna: I don't know. I just prefer a mixed team. It's easier for me to communicate.

Mark: I understand. By the way, have you heard the news about Yandex?

Anna: Yes. Bad news about Yandex created a bad atmosphere in our company. Everyone is nervous. People are looking for new jobs.

Mark: I know. In today's world we can't really plan anything. There are too many difficult problems.

Anna: Exactly. That's why I decided to leave. I want a quieter life. I don't miss this rush and long distances in Moscow.

Mark: But you're often late for things! How will you manage?

Anna: I know. I'm often late. But I never forget things. I'm too responsible. So I always finish what I start.

Mark: How do you deal with stress?

Anna: If I'm stressed, I make a list of things which I did. It helps me to stay active. My priority is to stay active and not to stop.

Mark: That's a good strategy.

Anna: I also believe: if I delay things I don't like, I won't develop. You need to get out of your comfort zone.

Mark: You're right. Good luck, Anna. I'll miss you.

Anna: Thank you, Mark. I'll miss you too. But it's time for a new chapter.
