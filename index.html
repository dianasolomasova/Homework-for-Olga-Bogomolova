The Event Planner

Chloe is an event planner. She organizes concerts and theater performances. Right now, she is preparing for a big music festival. She arranged a lot of meetings this week with musicians, technicians, and sponsors. Her schedule is crazy, so she has to write down every task in her planner to remember all her appointments.

Last year was very different. Chloe was looking for a job for 4 months. It was a hard time. She saw a position on a job website, applied for the job, and got it.

Chloe loves her job, but it is very stressful. Sometimes she thinks: "You will have a mental breakdown if you don't take a break." So she always tries to take at least one day off.

Chloe is usually a very organized person, but she hates one thing: housework. She always delays cleaning. She cleans only every two weeks because she simply doesn't have time for it.

Her friends say she works too much. But Chloe remembers her university days. Before her first big exam, she and her friends stayed up all night trying to learn all the theory in one night. Now she never stays up late to finish her tasks anymore.

This year, Chloe is also planning a trip to Italy with her boyfriend. They were supposed to fly tomorrow, but their flight was cancelled. So now they will fly in 2 days.

When they are in Italy, Chloe wants to forget about work completely. In Italy, she doesn't rush at all. She just drinks coffee and walks slowly. But at home in London, she and her boyfriend have a funny rule. They share time management with each other. And if they are late for a dinner, they are always late together.

After her vacation, Chloe will return to work. She already knows what she will do. She has new tools to increase ticket sales, and she is ready to make this festival the best one ever.
